# Privacy Policy: Blurr for WhatsApp Web

Last updated: 2026-09-27

Blurr is a browser extension that blurs parts of WhatsApp Web on your own screen, for screen sharing and privacy in public places.

## What Blurr collects
Nothing. Blurr has no server, no account, no analytics and no tracking.

## What Blurr stores, on your device only
- Your Blurr settings (which parts to blur, blur strength, reveal mode, idle time), in your browser's extension storage. If you use browser sync, your browser may sync these settings to your other devices.
- A short status of which parts are currently found on the page (for the lights in the Blurr window). No message text, names, numbers or pictures are stored.

## What leaves your device
Nothing. Blurr makes no network requests.

## What Blurr reads
Blurr changes how WhatsApp Web looks on your screen. It looks at the page structure (for example, where the chat list and messages are) to know what to blur. It does not read, copy, save or send your messages, contacts or media. It never changes what WhatsApp sends or receives.

## Permissions
| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Saves your Blurr settings |
| Access to `https://web.whatsapp.com/*` | Needed to apply the blur on WhatsApp Web. Blurr runs on no other site |
