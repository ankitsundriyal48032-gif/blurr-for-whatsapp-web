Blurr is on

Your WhatsApp Web chats are now blurred for screen sharing and prying eyes.

Get started

  1. Open or reload WhatsApp Web. Messages, chat previews, profile pictures and media are blurred from the first second.
  2. Hover over one item to read just that item. Move away and it blurs again.
  3. Before a screen share, press Alt+P. Presentation mode hides your whole chat list and blurs everything. Press Alt+P again after the call.
  4. Alone? Press Alt+B to turn Blurr off. Press it again to turn it back on.

The lights in the Blurr window

  • Green: found and hidden.
  • Safe mode: WhatsApp changed something. Blurr blurs the whole area to keep you safe until an update.
  • Area blurred: a part was not found. Its whole area stays blurred. Use "Copy problem report" to tell us.
  • Grey: nothing of that part is on screen right now.

One tip for screen sharing

Pop-up notifications come from your computer, not from the WhatsApp page, so no extension can blur them. Before you share your screen, turn them off in WhatsApp Web: Settings, then Notifications.

Your privacy

Blurr works only inside your browser. It uses no internet, reads no messages, sends nothing and collects no data. It only changes how WhatsApp Web looks on your screen, never what WhatsApp sends.

Shortcuts can be changed at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
